Become a sponsor

Help a student-led team represent Czechia on the world stage. Your support goes straight into robot parts, tools, registration fees and travel.

How you can help

  • Robot parts: motors, sensors, REV and VEX parts
  • 3D printing filament and tools
  • Registration fees for FTC, FGC and VEX
  • Flights and accommodation for competitions

What we give back

  • Your logo on our website
  • Your logo on our robot and T-shirts
  • Shout-outs on social media
  • Recognition as a lead partner
  • A visit to the Loft to drive a robot
