What we do

Three programs, one club

Throughout the year we design, build, program and drive robots in three international competition programs.

FGC

FIRST Global Challenge

The "robotics Olympics", with one team from each of more than 190 nations. ISP students have represented Czechia there since 2018: Mexico City, Dubai, Singapore, Athens, Panama, and Incheon next.

FGC history →
FTC

FIRST Tech Challenge

Team #11041. In 2016 we won the Inspire Award at the CEESA/FTC tournament in Prague, which sent us to the World Championship. Our most recent award is the Sustain Award in Hasselt, Belgium, in 2025.

FTC history →
VEX

VEX V5 · CEESA

Every February we compete against international schools from Central and Eastern Europe, most recently in Budapest (2025) and Tirana (2026).

VEX history →

Want to join?

Come to the Loft (Room 401) after school on a Tuesday or Friday. Anyone and everyone is welcome, and you don't need any experience. Builders, programmers, designers, writers and organisers all have a place on the team.
